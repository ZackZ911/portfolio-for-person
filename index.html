ABOUT ME

Grandfather taught me mahjong before I could do long division.

Since then I've played and studied nearly every card game I could find a table for — poker, blackjack, baccarat, pai gow, mahjong. What keeps me interested isn't the winning, it's the two things underneath every game: the probability that decides who wins in the long run, and the centuries of Chinese culture that shaped these games long before they reached a casino floor. I'm building toward a future in the casino industry — on the side that designs and understands the games, not just plays them.
